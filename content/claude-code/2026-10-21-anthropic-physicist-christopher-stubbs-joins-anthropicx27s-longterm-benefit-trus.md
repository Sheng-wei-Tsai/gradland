## Summary

Dr. Christopher Stubbs, an experimental physicist and former Dean of Science at Harvard University, has joined Anthropic’s Long-Term Benefit Trust. He brings to the Trust decades of scientific expertise across observational cosmology and precision measurement. He has also advised the US government on national security and arms control.

“I am honored to join Anthropic’s Long-Term Benefit Trust and to help the company deliver on its public benefit mission,” said Stubbs. “I use AI tools in my research every day and see what they make possible, and as an educator I have confronted some of the hard questions they raise. As a grandfather, I want to help ensure that AI is developed and deployed for the long-term benefit of humanity.”

Stubbs is the Samuel C. Moncher Professor of Physics and of Astronomy at Harvard, where he served as Dean of Science from 2018 to 2024. In 1998, as a member of the High-Z Supernova Search Team, he helped discover the accelerating expansion of the universe, work that later earned the team the Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics. He was also the inaugural project scientist for what is now the Vera C. Rubin Observatory, which started a 10-year survey of the southern sky in June. One of its aims is to study the accelerating expansion he helped discover.
